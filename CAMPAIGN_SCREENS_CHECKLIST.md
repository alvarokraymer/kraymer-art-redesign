## GIFs — pure animation, no footage needed

1. **Price counter build-up**: a pin appears, "$29". Two more appear, "3 pins = $79". Continues to 12, ends on "$279 = $23.25/pin". Uses your existing static renders, just staged entrances.
2. **Main swap**: 4 jewelry silhouettes on screen, each fades to a different main's piece every ~1.5s. Shows interchangeability without needing footage.
3. **Unlock progress bar**: horizontal bar fills left to right, pops an icon (necklace, ring, bracelet) at each threshold mark as it passes. Literally an animated version of the stretch-goal ladder.
4. **Orbit ring**: your 6 pins slowly rotating in a circle, one drifts forward to camera every 2s and holds with its name, then returns to orbit.
5. **Sketch to final crossfade**: lineart version of a piece crossfades into the finished colour render. Simulates process without needing real workshop footage.
