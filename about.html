Fine jewelry.

Kraymer
Founder & Designer

"I wanted jewelry I could wear anywhere, and still feel like myself."

I spent years teaching art and shipping pieces to more than 2,000 collectors before opening this store. Every commission taught me the same lesson: people did not want costume jewelry. They wanted something they could wear to a wedding, a work dinner, anywhere, and have it read as jewelry first.

That is the whole idea behind Kraymer. Every piece starts as a wax sculpture at the bench, not a die-cast mold. We work in solid 925 sterling silver and 18K gold, never brass or plated base metal, and we set real gemstones by hand.

I still approve every new design myself. If it is not something I would wear on my own hand, it does not ship.

Kraymer, Founder
