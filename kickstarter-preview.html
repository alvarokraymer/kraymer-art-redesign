Signals Between Stars: Enamel Pins Collection

Click below to collect a wide range of your favourite signals, at an exclusive discount & with freebies!

Campaign Rewards Creator FAQ Updates Comments Community

You deserve to celebrate! Made for the collectors who already know their main.

Join us in celebrating the signals between stars with a small constellation of enamel pins! (and one very dramatic comet)

Hi all! I'm Kraymer, you might recognise me from my socials where I'm grateful to have over 130k+ supporters! I've spent over 10 years as an artist, and with this collection, I wanted to create pins that would bring you happiness every time you looked at them, whilst also giving you a way to carry your favourite connection with you every day!

Furthermore, this batch will be a limited-edition version for Pride Month 2026!

This type of glow-in-dark effect is not easy to make (which is why so few enamel pins exist with glow other than green, I have gone through countless samples & tests in the past), so the prices will reflect that!

Once the campaign is over, you'll receive a survey where you will fill out which animal you want + shipping (so you don't need to choose the design right now! Just choose quantity. Also, you're free to choose multiple of 1 design. For example, for a 5 pin pledge, you could choose 3 foxes and 2 other pins)

The more funding milestones we hit, the more rewards for everyone!

So don't forget to spread the joy with the friends and family you have who would be interested in this campaign as well, so we can unlock more stretch goals together!

You'll also be able to add the bonus animals as post-campaign add-ons. So for everyone pledging 10 pins, you'll be able to complete the full collection through add-ons after the campaign!

Small business, not a corporation - whatever it takes to make sure you're happy, I'll do it out of my own pocket. Thousands of happy customers said it better than I can. I'd love for you to join them!

Shipping

I will be working with a shipping partner in Los Angeles to help make the shipping times and cost lower than they normally would be for deliveries to the USA! This will also lower the cost of shipping to other countries as well!

Shipping times (once all pins are finished & ready):

  • USA/Canada: 3-6 Days
  • Australia/New Zealand: 5-9 Days
  • Asia/Europe: 5-9 Days
  • Everywhere else: 10-20 Days

☆ All deliveries will include expedited shipping and tracking! ☆

Shipping costs and delivery times listed are estimates and may vary at specific location and other factors at the time of shipment. However, I've already included the packaging and supply costs in the overall funding goal to keep shipping charges as low as possible! The final charge you receive for shipment will also include other fees such as tariffs and taxes.

Risks and challenges

I've ran multiple successful Kickstarters now and have delivered thousands of pins to kind supporters! So I have quite a lot of experience now, and the risk for this type of project is quite minimal. I work with experienced, reputable & premium-priced manufacturers and will carefully inspect all items before shipping. I'll keep you updated every step of the way, and if any unexpected issues arise, I'll communicate them transparently through Kickstarter updates.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does Kickstarter say there's a risk? Will I receive my rewards?

Yes! While Kickstarter does give a warning to protect themselves, I can personally guarantee these will be fulfilled, as I have already arranged for samples for the pins, and I have extensive experience with developing enamel pins now, having made over 30+ designs, and having sold & delivered many, many pins on my stores to happy customers (as seen from reviews). The continuation of my small business matters a lot to me and I certainly would not want to deal with any legal troubles.

Are these available to ship to my country?

Yes! International shipping is available to just about every country (unless you live in Antarctica or North Korea).

What grading will the pins be?

All pins will be A grade! If somehow you receive a pin that is not A grade, I will send you a replacement for free.

What happens if my pins arrive damaged?

You can DM me with photos/videos about the situation and I can send a replacement for free!

Don't see the answer to your question? Ask the project creator directly.
