The Craft

Not Merch. Fine Jewelry.

Hand-Sculpted Originals

Every design starts as a wax sculpture at the bench, not a licensed asset pulled from a catalog. If it does not feel like the character, it does not ship.

Precious Metals & Gems

Solid 925 sterling silver and 18K gold. Hand-set stones. No brass, no nickel, nothing that turns your skin green.

Small Batches Only

We forge in numbered batches and stop when the batch is done. It keeps quality high and waste near zero.

From the founder

I spent years teaching art and shipping pieces to more than 2,000 collectors on Etsy and Kickstarter before opening this store. I started Kraymer Art because I wanted jewelry I could wear to a meeting and still feel like myself.

Every piece here is something I would put on my own hand. That is the whole standard.
