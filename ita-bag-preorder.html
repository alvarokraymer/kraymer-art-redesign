Why this bag exists

I kept seeing the same problem. Pins I loved with nowhere to put them, buried in a drawer instead of out in the world.

So I designed the bag I wanted for myself first. A clear window big enough for a full set, a strap that does not dig in, leather that does not crack after one summer.

Gojo and Geto sit on opposite sides of the flap, facing each other the way they never quite got to. Drawn here in the studio, not lifted from a screenshot.

One batch, by hand, in the order it is reserved. When the hundred are gone, this bag does not come back.
